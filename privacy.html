HiHatGo! – Privacy policy

Last updated: 15 November 2026

HiHatGo! is made by Next stop s. r. o. Contact: hihatgo@gmail.com.

What stays on your device. Your songs, charts, scores, practice history, settings and pictures are stored on your device. The app reads the hits from the USB-MIDI drum kit you connect; those stay on your device too. The app has no ads and no analytics, and sells nothing about you to anyone.

Cloud backup (optional). Only if you choose "Sign in with Google": the app uses Google Firebase (Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore) to back up and restore your progress. This sends your Google account's email address and name and a Firebase user ID (to sign you in); your scores, practice time and history; your settings and profile names; the titles and artists of the songs in your library (never the audio); and the profile pictures and album covers you chose. It travels encrypted (HTTPS) and is used only to back up and restore your progress.

Song info, covers and artist logos from the internet (optional, on by default). To show each song's proper name, album, year and cover, put albums in their real order and show band logos, the app looks up songs and artists in free public databases: MusicBrainz and the Cover Art Archive (run by the MetaBrainz Foundation), and Wikidata and Wikimedia Commons (run by the Wikimedia Foundation). Only artist names, song titles and the database's own record numbers are sent - never the audio, your name, email or any account. You can switch this off in Settings → Song info from the internet.

New songs from your favourite artists (optional). When you mark an artist as a favourite, the app asks the public chart website paradb.net whether new charts exist for that artist's name. Only the artist's name is sent.

Messages you send us (optional). Only when you press Send in Settings → Contact & feedback: your message, the kind you picked (idea / bug / other), the app version and your device's model and Android version, and any files you chose to add (photos, videos, screen recordings) are sent to the app's Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore and Cloud Storage). If you switch on "Add the app's log", the app's own technical log of the last minutes goes with it (what the app was doing, no personal data). If you are signed in, your account's email address is added so we can reply. It travels encrypted (HTTPS) and is used only to read and answer your message; ask hihatgo@gmail.com to have it deleted.

Fixes you share to help Chart Maker learn (optional, off until you say yes). Only if you turn on Settings → Help Chart Maker learn (the app also asks once, after your first saved fix): when you save changes in Fix notes on a Chart Maker song, the list of notes you changed (the time, the drum before and after) and, for each change, 3 seconds of that song's drum sound around it are sent to the app's Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore and Cloud Storage), only over Wi-Fi. Also sent: which Chart Maker version made the chart, the chart's file name (it contains the song's title) and the app version - never your name, email or account. It is used only to check the fixes and teach Chart Maker to hear drums better; every fix is checked before it is used. Turning the switch off stops sending and drops fixes still waiting on your device; ask hihatgo@gmail.com to have what was sent deleted.

Purchase and free trial. The free trial is counted on your device. If you buy the unlock, the payment is handled entirely by Google Play; HiHatGo! never sees your card or payment details, only whether the unlock has been bought. If you use the cloud backup, the date your trial started and whether you bought the unlock are kept with it, so reinstalling the app doesn't start the trial again.

Microphone (optional). Only if you turn on voice commands. Your speech goes to your device's speech-recognition service (usually Google's) to recognise commands like "play" or "volume up". HiHatGo! itself does not record, keep or send the audio.

Deleting your data. In the app: Profile → Cloud backup → Delete cloud backup deletes everything HiHatGo! backed up and signs you out. You can also email hihatgo@gmail.com and ask for your data and sign-in record to be deleted; this is done within 30 days. Uninstalling the app removes everything stored on your device.

Children. HiHatGo! is not directed at children under 13.

Changes. If this policy changes, the new version is posted on this page with a new date.
