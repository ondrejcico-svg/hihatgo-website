HiHatGo! tips and tricks

Everything HiHatGo! can do, and the quickest way to get to it. The same tips are inside the app under Settings → Tips, with pictures.

Playing songs

Read real drum sheet music

In a song: Visuals → Sheet music. Choose a page with a moving cursor or one scrolling line, dark or paper, all notes one colour or each drum in its own colour. Pinch to zoom. Notes you hit turn green, missed ones red.

Change the look while you watch

Song settings → Visuals. Tilt, Sheet, Color and Lights take the place of the other buttons, so the notes stay in sight while you change them.

Move a drum's column

Press and hold a column until the device buzzes, then slide it sideways. Your order is kept for every song, so the lanes can match how your kit is really set up.

Move and resize the wings

Press and hold a wing, then drag it: up or down slides it along the sheet, outwards or inwards makes it bigger or smaller. Don't want them? Settings → Visuals.

Turn the song's own drums off, or hear only them

Tap the drum button: full song, then drumless, then drums only. Drums only is great for hearing exactly what the drummer plays. Songs without a separate drum track can have their drums removed on the tablet by an AI.

Jam along with no notes

Tap the coloured difficulty next to the song's title and pick Jam. Play whatever you like on a concert stage. Nothing is judged and the time counts as free practice.

Notes not in time with the music?

Song settings → Timing: nudge the notes earlier or later, or tap Auto-detect. It changes every difficulty of the song at once.

Skip further with a few taps

Taps on the 10-second buttons add up: three quick taps jump 30 seconds. A long silent intro gets its own Skip button that lands you 3 seconds before the drums.

No notifications while you play

Settings → No notifications while you play. Messages wait until you leave the song, then your own Do Not Disturb settings come straight back.

Let a song pick your module's kit

Song settings → Kit: switch it on and pick a kit number. Your drum module changes to that kit sound whenever the song opens.

Loops and slow practice

Loop any part of a song

Tap the loop button: a big, zoomed-in seek bar opens over the sheet. Tap + at the start of the hard part, then + at the end. It repeats until you tap the loop button again.

Make a loop longer or shorter

Drag a green handle on the big seek bar, or use the green 1 buttons: back starts the loop a second earlier, forward ends it a second later. Hold them to take a second off.

Practise just the fills

Fills lists the song's fills, rolls and intros. Pick one and it loops with a short run-up; ‹ › step to the next fill.

Needs practice

Lists the parts you played nearly right, from this run or your last one. Those are exactly the parts a few loops can fix.

Practise from the Results screen

Scroll down under Back to Songs for Fills, Needs work and History. Swipe sideways to switch, tap a part to start looping it.

Speed-up trainer

Slow the song down under Speed and switch on "Speed up after clean loops". Each clean loop offers 5% more: tap OK, or just hit any drum to stay. Tap the 100% mark to snap back.

Exercises and progress

The Practice page

Rudiments, hi-hat, legs and grooves. Pick an exercise, a level and a tempo, then Start. It loops for as long as you like. Bright notes are accents (hit harder), dim ones are soft.

Open the next level

Tap Next level: 10 seconds with the notes, then fog covers them and you keep going by feel. Play a few clean rounds in a row (the whole test takes 30 seconds at most), then hit the snare twice quickly for the next level. No need to touch the screen.

How hard you hit, and the hi-hat pedal

Accents should be clearly louder than soft notes, and foot hi-hat notes played with the pedal. If the same mistake comes twice a word shows on the drum: Louder, Softer, Open it, Close it or Use the pedal. It's measured against your own hits, so a quiet kit works too.

Beat generator

Tap Generate for a brand-new groove or fill in rock, funk, disco, half-time, shuffle or fast. Add ghost notes, ride or open hi-hat, and keep the ones you like with the heart.

Track how clean your drills get

Profile → Practice history → Drills shows your mistakes in every session, per hand and for the feet. Expect lots on a new rudiment, then watch them drop.

Badges and medals

Big moments show as medals in the Practice history calendar: accuracy clubs, perfect hands and feet, flawless runs, practice streaks and hours. Tap a day to see them all.

Your practice time counts without a song

Just play the kit: your playing time is counted with the app open, in the background, or with the screen off. Open the app once after plugging in the kit.

Your song library

Add songs straight from a download

Open a downloaded Paradiddle .zip or Clone Hero .sng file with HiHatGo! and it's added to your library. No unzipping needed. Import also takes whole song folders.

Jam to any music file

Import → A music file without a chart: mp3, flac, m4a, ogg or wav. It plays in Jam, and Remove drums works on it too.

Song names, albums and covers by themselves

Real names, albums, track order, years and covers are looked up on the internet for you. Wrong one? Select the songs and tap Look up.

Favourites and shuffle

Tap the heart on a song, artist or album. Green shuffle means every song in the list comes once before any repeats, like a good music player.

Go to the artist or album

In a song, tap the cover, the title or the artist to jump to that artist's or album's page.

Select many songs at once

Press and hold a song, then drag over the others and use the bar that appears. Press and hold a folder to rename it or change its cover.

Your drum kit

Check your kit without a song

Settings → Drum kit lights up each drum you hit. Wrong drum? Tap the right one in the picture, tap Assign a pad and hit the pad. You can rename drums and change their colours there too.

Make the timing feel right

Settings → Audio & latency: run the delay calibration once, so every hit is judged where you see it. Avoid Bluetooth headphones while playing, they add delay.

Hear the song through your module

No Bluetooth on your module? Settings → Audio & latency → Song plays on → Drum module (USB cable). You hear the song and your drums together in the module's headphones, if your module takes sound over USB.

Guest mode

Letting a friend play? Profile → Guest. Their scores and practice time are kept apart from yours. Several real profiles on one tablet work too.
